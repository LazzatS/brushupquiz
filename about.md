# BrushUpQuiz

**Classroom quizzes, radically simple.**

Create quizzes in seconds — or let AI draft them for you. Students join with a code or a QR scan. Results appear the moment the timer stops. No accounts, no email, no fuss.

[**Get it on the App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/app/brushupquiz)

---

## Why BrushUpQuiz?

BrushUpQuiz was built for teachers who want to check understanding without wrangling logins, email invites, or subscription paywalls. Anonymous nicknames get everyone into a quiz in under 10 seconds.

- 📝 **Write or generate** — build questions by hand, or type a topic and let AI draft one for you
- 📡 **Live join codes** — students join instantly with a 6-character code or a QR scan
- ⏱ **Countdown timer** — set the time limit, and everyone answers within it
- 📊 **Real-time results** — see correct/incorrect distribution, plus each responder by name
- 🎨 **Modern design** — premium-glass UI, adapts to light and dark mode
- 🔒 **Privacy-first** — no email, no password, no tracking, no ads

---

## How it works

### For instructors
1. Sign in with a nickname
2. Create a quiz manually or with AI
3. Publish — a join code + QR appears
4. Watch responses stream in
5. Review results when the timer ends

### For students
1. Sign in with a nickname
2. Enter the join code or scan the QR
3. Pick your answer before the timer runs out
4. Get instant feedback

## Frequently asked

**Do students need accounts?**
No. Just a nickname on first launch.

**Do you collect email addresses?**
No. See our [Privacy Policy](https://lazzats.github.io/brushupquiz/privacy).

**Is it free?**
Yes, currently free with no in-app purchases and no advertising.

**Does it work offline?**
No — quizzes and results are synced over the internet, so a connection is required.

**What devices are supported?**
iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later.
